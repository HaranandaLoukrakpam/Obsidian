# [[Industrial Applications and Emerging Trends]]

High Performance Computing (HPC) has become an essential technology across industries that require massive computational power, real-time data analysis, and large-scale simulations. Modern HPC combines CPUs, GPUs, cloud computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and advanced networking to solve problems that are impossible or impractical for traditional computers.

Emerging technologies such as **Exascale Computing**, **Quantum Computing**, **Edge HPC**, and **Green Computing** are shaping the future of scientific research and industrial innovation.

# [[Quantum Computing Basics]]

Quantum Computing is a new computing paradigm based on the principles of quantum mechanics.

Unlike classical computers that use **bits (0 or 1)**, quantum computers use **qubits**, which can exist in multiple states simultaneously through **superposition**.

---

## [[Key Concepts]]

### [[Qubit]]

Basic unit of quantum information.

---

### [[Superposition]]

A qubit can represent both **0 and 1 simultaneously**, enabling many computations at once.

---

### [[Entanglement]]

Two or more qubits become linked, so the state of one instantly influences the others, even over large distances.

---

### [[Quantum Gates]]

Operations performed on qubits, similar to logic gates in classical computers.

---

## [[Applications]]

- Drug Discovery
    
- Cryptography
    
- Optimization Problems
    
- Financial Modeling
    
- Quantum Chemistry
    
- Artificial Intelligence
    

---

## [[Limitations]]

- Expensive hardware
    
- Error-prone qubits
    
- Difficult programming
    
- Limited commercial availability

# [[Exascale Computing]]

Exascale Computing refers to computing systems capable of performing at least **1 ExaFLOP (10¹⁸ floating-point operations per second).**

Exascale systems represent the next major milestone after petascale supercomputers.

---

## [[Characteristics]]

- Millions of processor cores
    
- GPU acceleration
    
- Massive parallelism
    
- Extremely high memory bandwidth
    
- Advanced cooling technologies
    

---

## [[Applications]]

- Artificial Intelligence
    
- Climate Modeling
    
- Molecular Simulation
    
- National Security
    
- Space Research
    
- Medical Research
    

---

## [[Challenges]]

- Power consumption
    
- Heat generation
    
- Fault tolerance
    
- Programming complexity
    
- Efficient communication

# [[Definition]]

An **[[Electrical Machine]]** is a device that converts energy from one form to another using the principles of [[Electromagnetism]].

Electrical machines are broadly classified into

- [[Transformer]]
- [[DC Machines]]
- [[AC Machines]]

---

# [[Electromechanical Energy Conversion]]

Electrical machines operate on the principle of converting

- Electrical Energy ↔ Mechanical Energy

using magnetic fields.

---

# [[Classification of Electrical Machines]]

## [[Static Machines]]

Machines without moving parts.

Example

- [[Transformer]]

---

## [[Rotating Machines]]

Machines having rotating components.

Examples

- [[DC Motor]]
- [[DC Generator]]
- [[Induction Motor]]
- [[Synchronous Motor]]

---

# [[Transformer]]

## [[Definition]]

A **[[Transformer]]** is a static electrical device that transfers electrical energy from one circuit to another through **[[Electromagnetic Induction]]** without changing the frequency.

---

## [[Working Principle]]

A transformer works on

### [[Faraday's Law of Electromagnetic Induction]]

A changing magnetic flux induces an electromotive force (EMF).

---

## [[Construction]]

Main parts

- [[Primary Winding]]
- [[Secondary Winding]]
- [[Magnetic Core]]
- [[Core Limbs]]
- [[Yoke]]

---

## [[EMF Equation]]

$$
E=4.44fN\phi_m
$$

where

- $E$ = Induced EMF
- $f$ = Frequency
- $N$ = Number of Turns
- $\phi_m$ = Maximum Magnetic Flux

---

## [[Turns Ratio]]

$$
\frac{V_1}{V_2}
=
\frac{N_1}{N_2}
$$

---

## [[Current Ratio]]

$$
\frac{I_1}{I_2}
=
\frac{N_2}{N_1}
$$

---

## [[Types of Transformers]]

### [[Step-Up Transformer]]

Increases voltage.

$$
N_2>N_1
$$

---

### [[Step-Down Transformer]]

Decreases voltage.

$$
N_2<N_1
$$

---

## [[Transformer Losses]]

- [[Copper Loss]]
- [[Iron Loss]]
- [[Hysteresis Loss]]
- [[Eddy Current Loss]]

---

## [[Efficiency]]

$$
\eta
=
\frac{Output}{Input}
\times100\%
$$

---

# [[DC Machines]]

## [[Definition]]

A **[[DC Machine]]** converts

- Mechanical Energy → Electrical Energy ([[DC Generator]])
- Electrical Energy → Mechanical Energy ([[DC Motor]])

---

# [[DC Generator]]

## [[Working Principle]]

Works on

### [[Faraday's Law]]

Mechanical rotation produces electrical energy.

---

## [[EMF Equation]]

$$
E_g
=
\frac{P\phi ZN}{60A}
$$

where

- $P$ = Number of Poles
- $\phi$ = Flux per Pole
- $Z$ = Conductors
- $N$ = Speed
- $A$ = Parallel Paths

---

## [[Applications]]

- Battery charging
- Electroplating
- Laboratories

---

# [[DC Motor]]

## [[Working Principle]]

Works on

### [[Lorentz Force]]

A current-carrying conductor placed inside a magnetic field experiences a force.

Force

$$
F=BIL
$$

---

## [[Back EMF]]

$$
E_b
=
V-I_aR_a
$$

---

## [[Types of DC Motors]]

- [[Series Motor]]
- [[Shunt Motor]]
- [[Compound Motor]]

---

### [[Series Motor]]

Characteristics

- Very high starting torque
- Speed decreases with load

Applications

- Electric trains
- Cranes
- Hoists

---

### [[Shunt Motor]]

Characteristics

- Nearly constant speed

Applications

- Fans
- Pumps
- Machine tools

---

### [[Compound Motor]]

Characteristics

- Combines advantages of Series and Shunt Motors.

Applications

- Elevators
- Press machines

---

# [[Induction Motor]]

## [[Definition]]

An **[[Induction Motor]]** is the most widely used AC motor.

It operates using

### [[Electromagnetic Induction]]

without any electrical connection to the rotor.

---

## [[Construction]]

Main Parts

- [[Stator]]
- [[Rotor]]
- [[Air Gap]]
- [[Frame]]

---

## [[Types of Rotors]]

- [[Squirrel Cage Rotor]]
- [[Slip Ring Rotor]]

---

## [[Slip]]

Slip is

$$
s
=
\frac{N_s-N}{N_s}
$$

where

- $N_s$ = Synchronous Speed
- $N$ = Rotor Speed

---

## [[Synchronous Speed]]

$$
N_s
=
\frac{120f}{P}
$$

---

## [[Advantages]]

- Rugged
- Reliable
- Low maintenance
- High efficiency

---

## [[Applications]]

- Pumps
- Compressors
- Fans
- Conveyors
- Industries

---

# [[Synchronous Motor]]

## [[Definition]]

A **[[Synchronous Motor]]** rotates exactly at synchronous speed.

$$
N=N_s
$$

Slip

$$
s=0
$$

---

## [[Applications]]

- Power factor correction
- Compressors
- Large industrial drives

# [[Electrical Protection Devices]]

---

## [[Fuse]]

Protects circuits from

- Overcurrent
- Short Circuit

Works by melting.

---

## [[MCB]]

[[Miniature Circuit Breaker]]

Automatically disconnects supply during faults.

Advantages

- Reusable
- Faster operation
- Safer than fuse

---

- Prevents electric shock
- Protects equipment

# [[Problem Solving Strategy]]

1. Identify the machine type.
2. Determine the operating principle.
3. Apply the appropriate formula.
4. Calculate efficiency or speed if required.
5. Analyze losses.
6. Verify units and practical limits.

---

# [[Common Mistakes]]

- Confusing generators with motors.
- Mixing synchronous speed and rotor speed.
- Forgetting that a [[Transformer]] operates only with AC.
- Using incorrect turns ratio.
- Assuming zero losses in practical machines.
- Confusing [[Step-Up Transformer]] and [[Step-Down Transformer]].

# [[Summary]]

[[Tags/Electrical Machines]] convert electrical and mechanical energy using the principles of [[Electromagnetism]] and [[Electromagnetic Induction]]. Major machines include the [[Transformer]], [[DC Generator]], [[DC Motor]], [[Induction Motor]], and [[Synchronous Motor]]. These machines are essential in power generation, transmission, industrial automation, transportation, and household appliances.

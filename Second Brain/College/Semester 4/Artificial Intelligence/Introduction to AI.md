# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## Definition

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is a branch of **Computer Science** that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, perception, understanding language, and recognizing patterns.

AI enables machines to analyze data, learn from experience, and make intelligent decisions with minimal human intervention.

---

## What is Intelligence?

**Intelligence** is the ability to:

- Learn from experience
- Solve problems
- Understand language
- Recognize patterns
- Adapt to new situations
- Make decisions
- Reason logically

Artificial Intelligence aims to replicate these abilities in machines.

---

## Goals of AI

The primary goals of AI are to:

- Simulate human intelligence.
- Automate repetitive tasks.
- Improve decision-making.
- Learn from data.
- Solve complex real-world problems.
- Increase efficiency and productivity.

## History of AI

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 1950 | [[Alan Turing]] proposed the [[Turing Test]]. |
| 1956 | The term **Artificial Intelligence** was coined by [[John McCarthy]] at the Dartmouth Conference. |
| 1960s–1970s | Early AI research and rule-based systems. |
| 1980s | Rise of expert systems. |
| 1997 | [[IBM Deep Blue]] defeated world chess champion [[Garry Kasparov]]. |
| 2011 | [[IBM Watson]] won the quiz show *Jeopardy!*. |
| 2012 | Deep Learning revolution in image recognition. |
| 2022–Present | Rapid growth of [[Generative AI]] and [[Large Language Model (LLM)|Large Language Models]]. |

## Types of AI

### Based on Capability

### [[Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)]]

- Also called **Weak AI**.
- Designed for a specific task.
- Most AI systems today belong to this category.

Examples:

- Siri
- Google Translate
- Spam filters
- Recommendation systems

---

### [[Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)]]

- Human-level intelligence.
- Can perform any intellectual task a human can.
- Still under research.

---

### [[Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)]]

- Intelligence beyond human capabilities.
- Hypothetical and not yet achieved.

---

## Based on Functionality

### [[Reactive Machine]]

- No memory.
- Responds only to current inputs.

Example:

- IBM Deep Blue

---

### [[Limited Memory]]

- Uses past experiences to improve decisions.
- Most modern AI systems belong here.

Examples:

- Self-driving cars
- Chatbots
- Recommendation systems

---

### [[Theory of Mind]]

- Would understand emotions, beliefs, and intentions.
- Still a research concept.

---

### [[Self-Aware AI]]

- Hypothetical AI with consciousness and self-awareness.
- Does not currently exist.

## AI Development Process

1. Collect data.
2. Prepare and clean the data.
3. Select an AI model.
4. Train the model.
5. Test and evaluate performance.
6. Deploy the model.
7. Monitor and improve the system.

## Advantages of AI

- Automates repetitive tasks.
- Processes large amounts of data quickly.
- Reduces human error.
- Operates continuously without fatigue.
- Improves productivity.
- Supports better decision-making.
- Enables personalized user experiences.

---

## Limitations of AI

- High development cost.
- Requires large amounts of quality data.
- Can inherit biases from training data.
- Lacks human emotions and common sense.
- Privacy and security concerns.
- Potential impact on employment.
- Ethical challenges.

## AI vs Human Intelligence

| Artificial Intelligence | Human Intelligence |
|--------------------------|--------------------|
| Learns from data | Learns from experience and reasoning |
| Very fast computation | Slower computation |
| No emotions | Emotional intelligence |
| Excels at repetitive tasks | Creative and adaptable |
| Requires training data | Can generalize from limited experience |
| Operates continuously | Needs rest |
